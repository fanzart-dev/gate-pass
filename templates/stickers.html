Box Stickers

One sticker per box, all identical. Prints onto SM Express stationery — only the three values go on the page, into the blanks beside the courier's own AWB No, ORIGIN and DESTINATION. Three labels to a 163 × 248mm sheet. Nothing is saved, and no gate pass number is used.
